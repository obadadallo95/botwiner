# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this research project respectful,
welcoming, and free from harassment, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

Use considerate language, assume good faith while checking evidence, give
feedback about the work rather than the person, and acknowledge uncertainty.
Research disagreements should be resolved with reproducible examples and clear
assumptions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted
contact, sexualized conduct, and publishing private credentials or personal
information are not acceptable. Attempts to use this project to harm others or
to conceal live trading risk are also out of scope.

## Enforcement

Report a conduct concern privately to the repository owner through the contact
route described in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and
addressed in proportion to the incident. Retaliation against a good-faith
report is not acceptable.
